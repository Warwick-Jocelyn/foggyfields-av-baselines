## LiDAR geometry — native LiDAR rasterization, held-out sweeps (99 per clip)

CD = symmetric mean nearest-neighbour distance between the rendered point cloud (ray-drop applied) and the GT sweep, in metres (the June-2026 definition; lower is better). CD² = nerfstudio/SplatAD's own `chamfer_distance` (sum of squared NN distances both ways / |GT|, m²). RCD = CD / mean GT range. Depth median L2 = median |pred − GT| range on returned rays. Ray-drop acc = ray-drop classification accuracy. June-2026 reference (NFF-02, original config): SplatAD CD 1.199 m / RCD 0.0487; NeuRAD 0.355 / 0.0144.

## LiDAR depth (camera-rendered expected depth vs GT returns, held-out frames, metres)

Caveat: SplatAD's camera depth is gsplat's alpha-weighted *expected* depth (`RGB+ED`). In fog, semi-transparent Gaussians near the camera dominate it, so it saturates at roughly 10 to 17 m frame-wide even where PSNR is fine (verified on faithful/NFF-02: pred/GT ratio 0.6 median, GT 20 to 40 m rendered at about 9.6 m). The numbers below therefore measure that saturation, not projection quality, and are similar under both conventions. The method's native LiDAR rasterization (ns-eval `median_depth`, used for the June CD/RCD study) is the meaningful geometry metric.
