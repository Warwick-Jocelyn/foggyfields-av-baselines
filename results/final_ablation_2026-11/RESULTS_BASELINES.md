Status: **IN PROGRESS** (updated as results arrive; see "Run log" at the end).

## Protocol (one evaluator for every row)
- Clips NFF-02 / NFF-08 / NFF-10, split `nvs_50_50` (train = even timesteps 0..198, eval = odd 1..197: 99 held-out timesteps).
- **RGB**: `jobs/eval_common_region.py` of the crop-ablation bench — common pixel set = the per-camera `safe_crop` rectangle of
  `calib/K_rectified_v2.json`, 7 cameras × 99 held-out frames = **693 images**; PSNR (data_range 1, mean over images), SSIM, LPIPS(alex).
  Baselines train with `--apply-safe-crop True --use-valid-masks False` (variant `safe`). The V1.3 lanes also train on the safe_crop
  images (their config has `apply_safe_crop: true`; `use_valid_masks: true` is a no-op inside the rectangle), so their renders are
  scored as variant `safe` too (1 px trim from `image_divisible_by`, within the evaluator's 8 px tolerance).
- **LiDAR** (held-out 99 sweeps, native LiDAR rays, LiDAR sensor frame): drop-independent protocol of `jobs/lidar_dump_ns.py`
  ("returns_only"): R = GT-returned rays (0 < range < 1000 m); one predicted point per ray = rendered range along the GT ray;
  **CD** = symmetric mean NN distance (m), **CD²** = (Σ_p min_g |p−g|² + Σ_g min_p |g−p|²)/|G| (m²), **depth median L2** =
  median |r_pred − r_gt| (m, not squared); JSON value = plain mean over sweeps. SplatAD / NeuRAD: `lidar_dump_ns.py`
  (raster / ray adapter); EmerNeRF: `jobs/emernerf/lidar_dump_emernerf.py` + `lidar_metrics_from_dump.py`; V1.3:
  `jobs/final/v13_score.py` (api `range` = detected hard-peak range incl. fog echoes) + `lidar_metrics_from_dump.py`.
  Our model has no ray-drop head: no drop is predicted ("oracle drop" = scored on the GT return set, like EmerNeRF). NeuRAD /
  SplatAD additionally have drop-aware numbers (ray-drop acc.) in their JSONs; they are not in the table.
- **Points kept** = Σ GT returns scored (n_gt in the LiDAR JSON).
